Rice cooker Overnight Oats:

Hearty mix of steel cut oats and 10 grain cereal, cooked overnight in a rice cooker. Add all ingredients into the rice cooker, stir, and set to cook on porridge setting for 1:40. When done, stir and let sit for a few minutes.

Portion multiplier:

2 large servings oatmeal.

0.5 cups steel cut oats.
2 cups water.
0.25 cups 10 grain cereal.
0.3 cups oat milk.
6 grinds salt.
0.3 cups raisins.
honey to taste.
apples and cinnamon, or vanilla to taste.

When making 2 servings, the amount of liquid comes up to the mark 2 white rice / 0.25 porridge. After adding raisings, liquid rises to the mark 2 brown rice.
