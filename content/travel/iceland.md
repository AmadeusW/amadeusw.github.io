## Highlands
Thorsmork. Checkout the Volcano huts and hikes there. We took a 4x4 semi-amphibous bus to get there, as you need to ford a few rivers to get there. The scenery was breathtaking, and it's a destination I would like to get back to and spend a few days there next time.

# Reykjavik
In Reykjavík, go to “meet the natives” in the Tin Can Factory. It takes ~3 hours and you get intro to culture, languages, history and cuisine. They serve good home made food!

Thats so exciting you're going to Iceland!  You'll love it.  What you have planned so far is what I would recommend.  The south coast is definitely where you get to see the most if you only have a few days.  If I was you I'd probably go as far east as Hofn. It's a bit of a rough drive with some river crossings and things like that but it's doable in a big vehicle.  You're not allowed to go up unless you have four wheel drive.  If you do that though make sure you check the road conditions map to see which roads are actually open

Some notable things along the south coast are Seljalandsfoss, Skogafoss, the plane crash on the black sand beach (you can google it to find directions), Vik black sand beach, and the Fjadrargljufur Canyon.  There's also a lot of "elf houses" along the way that are cool to look at.  A lot of the local people believe in elves and build little houses for them and things like that so its interesting to see and read about.  There's also two really cool natural hot springs along the south coast, the first is about a 1 - 1.5 hour hike to a hot river from a town called Hveragerdi, and the second is to the oldest outdoor swimming pool in Iceland called Seljavallalaug.  Its about a half hour hike from the place that you can park.  And again you can google any of these things to find the actual directions because I can't remember off hand
Lindsay
Reykjavik is a really cool city and is always changing so there's nothing really specific I'd recommend there, just walk around and explore everywhere and pop in all the shops.  They also have really really good restaurants so definitely eat at some of those. They do a free guided walking tour of the city which I recommend doing too, I really enjoyed it when I did it.
