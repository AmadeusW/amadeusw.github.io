Rice cooker **Overnight Oats**:

Hearty mix of steel cut oats and 10 grain cereal, cooked overnight in a rice cooker.
Add all ingredients into the rice cooker, stir, and set to cook on **porridge** setting for **1:40**.
When done, stir and let sit for a few minutes.
