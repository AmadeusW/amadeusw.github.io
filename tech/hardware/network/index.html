Network Overview & Rules

  • VLANs & Routing: The pfSense router creates separate VLANs:
    • Private VLAN: Unrestricted WAN internet access and full routing access to all other VLANs.
    • IoT / Guest VLAN: Outbound WAN internet access only; blocked from accessing other VLANs.
    • Camera VLAN: No access to internet or any other part of network. Accessible from Private VLAN.
    • TV VLAN: No access to internet or any other part of network.
  • Wired Switch: All wired switch ports connect directly into the Private Network (hosting Synology NAS, Home Assistant, Printer, and Computers).
  • Access Point: Broadcasts distinct SSIDs corresponding to all 4 VLANs and tags packet for correct routing.
  • Wireless Devices: Their data is tagged according to which SSID they joined.
